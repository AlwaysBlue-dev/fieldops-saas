## Where Housecall Pro is stronger

Housecall Pro is built to help home service businesses win and bill work. You get online booking from Google or your website, estimates, invoicing, card and bank payments, and consumer financing. It adds review management, a price book, and flat-rate pricing, plus a large add-on catalog: AI call answering, payroll, websites, vehicle GPS and dashcams. It has a native mobile app in English and Spanish with offline viewing, and integrations with QuickBooks, Google, and Thumbtack. FieldKeel doesn't offer invoicing, payments, or marketing, and doesn't pretend to.

## Where FieldKeel wins

**Flat yearly pricing with room for a crew.** FieldKeel publishes three annual plans: Starter at $299/year (5 users), Professional at $499/year (10 users), and Business by quote for larger teams (up to 100 users). On Housecall Pro, MAX includes 8 users at $299/month billed annually, and each extra user is $35/month. Add-ons are priced separately. FieldKeel's published plans keep the budget conversation short.

**Dispatch, crews, time, and approvals in one view.** FieldKeel's dispatch board shows today's work, which crews are in the field, and which jobs need a decision. Clock sessions and review queues sit next to the jobs they belong to, so approving hours happens in context.

**No app store required.** Your team installs FieldKeel from the browser on a phone, tablet, or desktop. Onboarding a new hire or a seasonal crew means sending a link.

**Designed for hands-on field work.** Bottom navigation, large tap targets, and keyboard-safe forms make it usable on a phone on a job site. Each person gets a My Day view, and the office gets a dense desktop layout with jobs, schedules, and sites.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Housecall Pro if:**
- You want booking, estimates, invoicing, payments, and financing in one system.
- Online reviews, marketing campaigns, and lead follow-up are a priority.
- You need QuickBooks sync or bundled services like payroll and bookkeeping.
- You're a residential home service business focused on growing customer volume.

**Choose FieldKeel if:**
- Billing and accounting already work in another system, and you need better control over daily operations.
- You coordinate multiple crews and need time approvals next to the work.
- You want published annual plans rather than per-user and add-on fees.
- You want field staff on a web app without app store installs.
- You run a mid-size HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest, landscaping, or facilities operation.
