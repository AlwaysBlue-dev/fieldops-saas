# FieldKeel vs Jobber: A Jobber Alternative Built Around Dispatch, Crews, and Time

Jobber is one of the best-known names in home service software, and for good reason. It covers quoting, invoicing, payments, client communication, and marketing in one product. But if your daily headache is running crews, not sending invoices, you may be paying for a lot you don't use.

FieldKeel is a field service and operations management platform that keeps **dispatch, crews, time, and approvals in a single workspace**. It's dense on desktop for the office and app-like on a phone for the field. Published annual plans: **Starter $299/year (5 users, 5 GB)**, **Professional $499/year (10 users, 20 GB)**, and **Business (contact sales; up to 100 users, 250 GB)**.

Here's an honest look at how the two compare.

## Where Jobber is stronger

Let's be straight: Jobber does more. If you want one tool that takes a customer from online booking to quote to invoice to paid, Jobber is built for that. It handles credit card and ACH payments, syncs with QuickBooks Online and Xero, and offers an app marketplace with 100+ integrations. It also has marketing tools, AI features, and a large user community. FieldKeel doesn't try to compete on any of those.

## Where FieldKeel wins

**A price you can plan around.** FieldKeel publishes three annual plans: Starter at $299/year (5 users), Professional at $499/year (10 users), and Business by quote for larger teams (up to 100 users). On Jobber, a 10-user team on Grow pays $299/month billed annually, and every added user costs $29/month. For an ops team trying to keep software costs predictable, that difference matters.

**Dispatch, crews, time, and approvals together.** FieldKeel is built around the operations loop: what's on today, which crews are out, which jobs need a call, and whose hours need review. Clock sessions and approval queues sit next to the jobs they belong to. Nobody has to cross-check a separate timesheet screen.

**No app store rollout.** Crews install FieldKeel straight from the browser on a phone, tablet, or desktop. No app store accounts and no waiting on downloads. If someone gets a new phone, they open a link.

**Built for the field, not just shrunk for it.** Bottom navigation, large targets, and keyboard-safe forms mean techs in gloves or on a ladder can actually use it. The office gets a dense desktop view, and each person has a My Day view of their own work.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Jobber if:**
- You need quoting, invoicing, and payment collection in the same tool as scheduling.
- QuickBooks Online or Xero sync is a must.
- You want built-in client communication, online booking, and marketing.
- You're a solo operator or small team that values an all-in-one front office.

**Choose FieldKeel if:**
- You already handle invoicing and accounting somewhere else and want a sharper operations tool.
- Your main pain is coordinating crews, tracking time, and approving hours.
- You want published annual plans instead of per-plan, per-user math.
- You'd rather skip app store installs for your field team.
- You run a mid-size crew in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest, landscaping, or facilities and the office lives on the dispatch board.

## FAQ

**Is FieldKeel a full replacement for Jobber?**
Not for every business. FieldKeel covers dispatch, crews, time, approvals, schedules, sites, and My Day. It doesn't list quoting, invoicing, or payments on its site. If you rely on Jobber for billing, you'd keep a separate tool for that.

**How much does FieldKeel cost compared to Jobber?**
FieldKeel Starter is $299/year (5 users, 5 GB), Professional is $499/year (10 users, 20 GB), and Business is contact sales (up to 100 users, 250 GB). Jobber's plans range from $29/month (Core, 1 user, billed annually) to $529/month (Plus, 15 users, billed annually), plus $29/month for each added user.

**Do my techs need to download an app?**
No. FieldKeel installs from the browser on a phone, tablet, or desktop. You don't need the App Store or Google Play.

**Does FieldKeel track crew time?**
Yes. Clock sessions and review queues live next to the jobs they belong to, so the office can review and approve time in context.

**Can I try FieldKeel before paying?**
Yes. FieldKeel offers a 14-day free trial.
