# FieldKeel vs ServiceTitan: A ServiceTitan Alternative for Mid-Size Shops

ServiceTitan is a heavyweight in trades software. It describes itself as software for commercial and residential trades, covering dispatch, call booking, invoicing, pricebook, payroll, memberships, marketing attribution, and deep reporting. Large, growth-focused contractors get a lot out of it.

Many mid-size shops don't need all of that. They need the dispatch board to work, crews to know where they're going, and hours approved without a fight. That's what FieldKeel is built for. It's a field service and operations management platform that keeps **dispatch, crews, time, and approvals in a single workspace**: dense on desktop, app-like in the field. Pricing is published: **Starter $299/year (5 users, 5 GB)**, **Professional $499/year (10 users, 20 GB)**, and **Business (contact sales; up to 100 users, 250 GB)**.

## Where ServiceTitan is stronger

ServiceTitan is far broader. It handles the front office (call booking, call recording, marketing attribution) and the back office (invoicing, pricebook, payroll, commissions, accounting). Its reporting tracks close rates and technician performance, and it supports memberships and mobile estimates. It also covers construction projects as well as service visits. If you're a large residential or commercial contractor running a call center, sales-driven technicians, and memberships, ServiceTitan's depth is a real advantage. FieldKeel doesn't offer those features.

## Where FieldKeel wins

**You can see the price.** ServiceTitan's pricing isn't public. You book a demo and get a per-technician quote. FieldKeel lists plans plainly: Starter $299/year (5 users), Professional $499/year (10 users), and Business by quote for larger teams (up to 100 users). You can budget for Starter or Professional before you talk to anyone.

**Start in days, not after a sales cycle.** With FieldKeel you start a 14-day free trial and put your real jobs, crews, and schedules on the board. Your crews install the app from the browser, with no app store needed.

**Focused on operations.** FieldKeel does a few things well. The dispatch board shows today's work, crews in the field, and jobs that need a decision. Clock sessions and review queues stay next to the jobs they belong to. Jobs, schedules, sites, and each person's My Day round it out. You aren't paying for a call center, marketing suite, or memberships engine you won't use.

**Field-friendly by design.** Bottom navigation, large targets, and keyboard-safe forms make FieldKeel easy to use on a phone at a job site. The office gets a dense desktop layout.
